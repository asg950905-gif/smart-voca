# 형식1) 영단어|뜻            -> 뜻만 교체 (뜻이 겹치는 단어를 구분하기 위함)
# 형식2) 영단어|뜻|품사|예문   -> 통째로 교체 (예문에 표제어가 그대로 없을 때)
# ---- 예문에 표제어가 없던 항목 (활용형 -> 원형으로 다시 씀) ----

forge|위조하다|verb|He tried to forge a signature.
signatory|서명인|noun|Each signatory must attend the meeting.
authority|당국|noun|The city authority closed the road.
apply|적용되다|verb|The discount will apply to all items.
investigate|수사하다|verb|Police will investigate the theft.
certify|증명하다|verb|The doctor will certify that he is fit to work.
disqualify|자격을 박탈하다|verb|Late forms will disqualify the applicant.
property|부동산|noun|The firm owns property in three cities.
steal|훔치다|verb|Thieves steal laptops from offices.
modify|변경하다|verb|We will modify the design to cut costs.
classify|구분하다|verb|We classify the goods by size.
amenity|편의 시설|noun|Free parking is the best amenity of the hotel.
grocery|식료품점|noun|The grocery opens at seven.
allergy|알레르기|noun|Please tell us about any food allergy.
sweep|쓸다|verb|Please sweep the floor before closing.
occupy|차지하다|verb|The firm will occupy three floors.
study|공부하다|verb|She will study English every night.
identify|식별하다|verb|The staff will identify the cause of the problem.
overcome|극복하다|verb|The firm will overcome many obstacles.
unify|통합시키다|verb|The firm will unify its brands.
